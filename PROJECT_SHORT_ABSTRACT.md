# SHORT ABSTRACT FOR COLLEGE SUBMISSION

## ⚡ Short Abstract (Concise Version - 1 Paragraph)

**CareerMatch AI** is an intelligent web application built using **React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS** that automates candidate resume screening and company job matching. Upon uploading a resume (PDF/DOCX/TXT), the system automatically parses candidate skills, experience, and contact details to calculate a real-time **ATS Score (0–100%)**. It matches candidate qualifications against company job openings, evaluating degree and skill requirements to deliver an automated eligibility decision (**`PASS`**, **`PASS (NEEDS IMPROVEMENT)`**, or **`FAIL`**). The platform also features an **Interview Prep Studio**, a **Candidate History Database**, an embeddable **Career Bot Widget**, and single-page **PDF Export & Emailing**.

## 📌 Super Crisp 4-Bullet Summary (Perfect for PPT Slides)

- **Problem Addressed**: Manual resume screening is slow, and candidates struggle to find applicable company job roles.
- **Core Solution**: An AI-powered web app that extracts resume skills, calculates ATS compatibility, and matches candidates to company roles.
- **Key Output**: Instant ATS Score (0-100%) and eligibility decision (`PASS`, `PASS (NEEDS IMPROVEMENT)`, or `FAIL`) with tailored interview questions.
- **Tech Stack**: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, pdfjs-dist, jsPDF, LocalStorage API.
