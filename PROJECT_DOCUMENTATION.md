## 📄 3. College Project Abstract (Copy & Paste Ready)

**Project Title**: CareerMatch AI: Automated Resume Analyzer, ATS Scorer & Enterprise Career Bot System  
**Domain**: Advanced Web Technology (AWT) / Full Stack Web Development  
**Technology Stack**: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, pdfjs-dist, jsPDF, LocalStorage API  

### **Abstract**
In the modern hiring ecosystem, job seekers face significant hurdles in navigating company career pages, manually identifying applicable job roles, and evaluating their resume compatibility against varying company eligibility criteria. Simultaneously, recruitment teams spend excessive manual hours screening unstructured resumes. To address these challenges, we present **CareerMatch AI**, an intelligent, web-based candidate evaluation and automated job-matching platform.

Built using a modern web architecture (**React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS**), CareerMatch AI automates the end-to-end recruitment evaluation workflow. The system enables candidates to upload resumes in PDF, DOCX, or text format. An automated client-side parsing engine extracts candidate names, technical skill matrices, experience levels, and contact details while calculating real-time **Applicant Tracking System (ATS) compatibility scores (0–100%)**.

The core matching engine cross-references candidate qualifications against company-specific job openings, evaluating degree requirements, experience thresholds, and skill overlap to deliver an explicit selection decision (**`PASS (SELECTED)`**, **`PASS (NEEDS IMPROVEMENT)`**, or **`FAIL (NOT ELIGIBLE)`**). Key system modules include:
1. **Resume Analyzer & ATS Audit Engine**: Provides keyword density scoring, formatting checks, and action verb recommendations.
2. **Automated Company Job Matcher**: Displays role fit percentages and eligibility criteria checklists for top enterprise roles.
3. **Interactive Interview Prep Studio**: Generates role-tailored technical, system design, and behavioral questions with STAR framework answer benchmarks.
4. **Persistent Candidate Database**: Local storage bank allowing HR/evaluators to track saved candidate records and history.
5. **Embeddable Enterprise Career Bot Widget**: Lightweight script allowing any company to embed the career bot widget directly into their career page.
6. **1-Page PDF Generator & Email Dispatcher**: Instant single-page PDF report compilation and direct email dispatching.

**Keywords**: Applicant Tracking System (ATS), Resume Parsing, Job Matching Engine, Web Application Development, Candidate Database, Interview Studio.
